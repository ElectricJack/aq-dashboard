## Method and environment

- **References.** The 12 mockups in `docs/design/*.png` (see `docs/design/manifest.json`), each against its `docs/specs/0002-product-design.md` section. Settings has no mockup and was checked against the spec (§2/§3/§9/§10 and `docs/specs/0001-dashboard.md`).
- **Sizes.** Desktop 1440x900 (`-d`) and phone 390x844 with touch (`-m`) are the primary comparisons. Tablet notes use 1024x768 (`-l`) and 768x1024 (`-t`). Some pages were also spot-checked at 320 wide.
- **Stack.**
  - The live stack at `http://127.0.0.1:18080` was **down** for the whole audit (connection reset, then HTTP 000). There are no live captures.
  - Every capture comes from `vite --mode mock` (MSW, marked "Mock data · proposed contracts").
  - The mock leaves out `terminal.*`, `results.*`, `playbooks.*` and `projects.read`, and some `tasks.*` / `decisions.resolve`. Where it does, the audit used browser-only fetch shims to review the real layouts. Shim data is labelled **AUDIT FIXTURE**.
  - No source file was changed.
- **Evidence.** `docs/evidence/gap-audit-2026-11-03/`, as JPEG at quality 72. `<name>-{d,l,t,m}.jpg` are the per-page baselines; prefixed captures show states and interactions. No baseline has horizontal page overflow at any size.
- **Owner.** UI means the fix fits inside aq-dashboard. SERVER means it needs an aq-server contract; those are listed in the cross-repo section and are not filed here. COVERED means it is already in the scope of an existing task.
- **Severity.**
  - *blocking*: blocks daily-driver use, or covers or hides content.
  - *important*: a visible deviation from the mock's hierarchy, density or controls.
  - *polish*: everything else.
