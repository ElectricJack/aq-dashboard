## Review and execution rulings

The executing-plans skill required a fresh final read-only reviewer, run on GPT-6 Astra high against application head `b3fee5e` plus the later browser-test delta. No Critical findings and no Minor findings were raised. Three Important findings were accepted and fixed in one regression-led pass: unchanged legal labels must not be split during title-only edits; unrelated labels must not block non-label actions; expired operation receipts must retain their original identity without claiming rejection or permitting a new-ID recovery. Lookup and exact-retry retirement both have regressions.

Other execution rulings: (1) execute the authorized task in-session, without a redundant plan-approval pause; (2) use scoped pnpm/Vitest because `aq test` invokes pytest in this JS repository; (3) preserve cached record content after a failed post-confirmation refresh, visibly label the old revision and retain the receipt; (4) run the copied upstream checker without editing it and pin its matching read schema separately. The tradeoffs are scoped test coverage, visibly stale content until refresh, and one additional maintained schema pin. These do not create a second writable lifecycle.
